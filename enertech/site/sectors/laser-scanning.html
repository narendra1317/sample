Laser Scanning & 3D Modelling

Precise, efficient and non-invasive capture of onshore and offshore structures, turned into 3D models that support asset management, maintenance, retrofit, modification and future development.

Our equipment

NavVis VLX wearable mobile mapping

In December 2024 we invested in the NavVis VLX and the NavVis IVION platform. Its four-camera setup captures high-resolution 360° imagery and highly accurate point clouds, and its portability keeps time on site to a minimum.

IVION turns raw point clouds into immersive, web-based digital twins your whole team can access remotely. In February 2025 our team took it to the Middle East for a site survey.
