Oil & Gas

Hydrocarbons will be part of the energy mix for years to come. We help operators and duty holders keep ageing assets safe, compliant and productive, while cutting the emissions that come with them.

01 / Onshore & offshore

Brownfield modifications

Brownfield projects keep mature infrastructure relevant and efficient, letting you adapt to new challenges without building new. Whether a modification is for capacity, a technology upgrade or regulatory compliance, our construction- and fabrication-led design meets the requirements of your technical authorities and site operations team from the outset.

We design fit for purpose, coordinating every discipline. Our designs account for ease of construction, fabrication, transportation and lifting, and we specify locally available materials where we can to reduce the carbon footprint.

  • Concept, FEED & detailed design
  • Multidiscipline 3D modelling
  • Capacity & technology upgrades
  • Regulatory compliance modifications
  • Approved fabricator network
  • End-to-end, from survey to installation
02 / Onshore & offshore

Repair orders

Repair orders fix or replace damaged or degraded equipment, structures and systems before they compromise safety or production. Whether it's a spool, pipe supports, handrails or gratings, we take each repair from inspection finding to installed solution.

  • Holistic review of inspection reports & surveys to prioritise repairs
  • CTRs to budget fabrication, construction & installation
  • Investigation of survey data, drawings & scans
  • Fit-for-purpose repair design
  • Fabrication quality checks with approved fabricators
  • TQ/SQ responses & offshore construction support
03 / Onshore & offshore

Decommissioning

Our engineers and designers support the safe decommissioning of assets, from first feasibility study to approval of the contractor's design. Where it adds value, we pair physics-based structural twins from our partner Akselos with virtual HSE services to de-risk removal planning.

  • Feasibility studies
  • Structural assessment of existing decks & steelwork
  • LOLER lifting plans & lifting design
  • Review & approval of contractor designs
  • 3D modelling
  • Digital twin & virtual HSE support
04 / Emissions reduction

Flare & power decarbonisation

UK operators and duty holders are under pressure to cut emissions from producing platforms by 2030. Power generation and flaring typically account for most of a platform's carbon footprint, so they are where the biggest gains are.

70%+of platform emissions typically come from power and flare
2030UK target date for emissions cuts on live platforms
ORSour process safety partner for decarbonisation studies

Working with our process safety partner ORS Consulting, we identify and engineer practical options, including:

  • Gas capture & utilisation
  • Gas recovery technologies
  • Electrification & hybrid power systems
  • Energy storage solutions
  • Waste heat recovery
  • Energy efficiency improvements
