Process engineering & design

Mechanical engineering (rotating & static)

Analysis, conceptual and detailed design, material selection, simulation and testing, manufacture, installation and commissioning, and maintenance and monitoring.

  • Pumps, compressors & turbines
  • Fans, blowers & gearboxes
  • Pressure vessels & heat exchangers
  • Reactors & storage tanks
  • PED calculations
  • Downhole completion tools

Structural engineering & design

  • Static, dynamic & FEM analysis

  • Lifting design
  • Material specifications

  • Drawings & calculations
  • Fatigue assessment (DNV-RP-C203)
  • Weight limit & laydown analysis

Electrical engineering & design

  • System specification & design
