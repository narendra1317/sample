About EnerTech Synergies

Synergising technologies into optimum solutions.

We're an Aberdeen-based team of thinkers, engineers and designers. Since 2014 we've helped clients across every engineering discipline deliver sustainable project value, working in partnership so that the solutions exceed expectations.

Our story

From one contractor to a multidisciplinary consultancy.

EnerTech's story began with a single self-employed contractor. Today we're a team of more than 20 full-time employees and contractors.

We owe that growth to treating people not as resources but as key members of the company. We follow a simple 3R policy, Respect, Retain and Reward, and work hard to create a fun, enjoyable and sustainable environment where great engineers do their best work.

Energy is in transition, and we help our clients adapt. We work with everyone from global developers to technology start-ups, from straightforward structural upgrades to innovative technology that will accelerate the energy transition.

Message from the Managing Director

Welcome to EnerTech Synergies.

Ramesh Kumar CEng, FICE · Managing Director

At EnerTech Synergies we pride ourselves on delivering innovative and sustainable engineering solutions for the oil, gas and renewable energy sectors. Our industry is evolving rapidly, and we are committed to staying ahead of the curve.

Our vision is to be at the forefront of this transformation, bridging the gap between traditional energy sources and renewable alternatives, and delivering engineering solutions that are both economically viable and environmentally responsible.

We believe engineering excellence is not just about solving problems but about anticipating them and turning challenges into opportunities. We see our client relationships as partnerships built on trust, transparency and shared goals, and every project is tailored to the client's needs.

Thank you for visiting. We look forward to working with you and contributing to a more sustainable and energy-efficient future.

Vision & mission

What drives us

Vision

To be the leading provider of innovative and transformative engineering services, empowering businesses to achieve sustainable growth and excellence while contributing to the development of communities and the empowerment of youth.

Mission

To provide simple, smart, sustainable solutions in the energy and infrastructure sectors, driven by our talented team, cutting-edge engineering and a relentless pursuit of excellence.

Our values

Guided by PASSION.

Seven principles that shape our culture, the way we work with clients and the way we treat each other.

P

People-centric

We put employees, customers and community at the heart of every decision, listening actively and tailoring solutions to real needs.

A

Accountability

We take responsibility for our actions and answer to clients, community and stakeholders, with transparency, ethics and fairness.

S

Service excellence

Bespoke engineering using digital twins, AI and physics-based tools to produce simple solutions that are easy to fabricate and build, with safety at the core.

S

Sustainability

Environmental, economic and social sustainability, from cutting our own footprint to inspiring young people and supporting local causes.

I

Integrity

Honest, consistent and compliant, keeping promises even when that means giving up short-term gains.

O

Ownership

Our people take pride in their work, own their decisions and outcomes, and hold each other to our values.

N

Novelty

Creativity and bold ideas. Quality is our standard, but delight is our goal.
