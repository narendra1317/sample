Corporate social responsibility

Our commitment to community and sustainability.

Giving back drives everything we do, whether that's empowering our clients, enriching our community or protecting our environment.

On this page Young engineers Environment Wellbeing Giving back Mentoring

01 · Engaging the young workforce

Inspiring the next generation of engineers.

We work with schools, colleges and universities, giving students small research projects that match real industry needs and bridge the gap between academia and industry.

We run workshops on the industry's move to renewables and digitalisation, and we're building partnerships with Robert Gordon University, the University of Aberdeen and institutions overseas to stay at the forefront of new technology.

July 2025: At ‘Engage with Engineers’ in Boddam, hosted by the 2nd Battalion The Highlanders Army Cadet Force, our stand ran a paper plane competition and a vernier calliper challenge using 3D-printed models.

Taking responsibility for our own footprint.

We build sustainability into our core business strategy and measure our carbon footprint and energy use so we can keep improving.

03 · Health is wealth

Healthy bodies, healthy minds.

Physical and mental wellbeing go hand in hand. Regular exercise, a balanced diet and an enjoyable work culture all support clear thinking.

  • Walk or cycle to work, and walk at lunch
  • Encouraged breaks after every hour of work
  • Fresh fruit in the office
  • Community wellness events such as the Kiltwalk

04 · Giving back

Supporting Aberdeen's communities.

We believe everyone has a role in building stronger, more resilient communities. We support local food and children's charities through regular donations and fundraising events.

  • Volunteering and fundraising with Aberdeen Cyrenians
  • Fundraising for local food banks, including CFINE
  • Supporting AberNecessities, so no child goes without
  • Donating food and essentials through local community groups
  • Sponsoring community sports, local charities and start-ups

05 · Professional mentoring

Helping our people thrive.

Our mentoring programme pairs people with experienced leaders, matched on career goals, skills and interests, for short- or long-term commitments.

Regular check-ins, in person and virtual, keep development on track, covering skills, leadership coaching and career progression. We encourage everyone to maintain CPD and work towards professional qualifications such as chartership and safety professional status.
