02 / Performance

Hydrogen plant optimisation

We optimise hydrogen plants to improve efficiency, cut energy consumption and lift overall performance. Through advanced process design, digital monitoring and in-house simulation software, we help you maximise hydrogen yield, lower operating costs and stay compliant.

Featured research: At All-Energy 2025 our team presented work on minimising the levelised cost of hydrogen (LCOH) for an off-grid green hydrogen plant, using scenario-based design, AI and renewable energy forecasting.

03 / Renewables

Offshore wind

Offshore wind is central to the global transition to sustainable power. Our experienced engineers and designers carry out complex analysis with the latest software, designing to the relevant codes and standards so projects are technically and economically viable and meet regulatory and environmental requirements.

We work with clients from the start of a project through the full design cycle, bridging the gap between technical challenges and strategic decisions.

  • Site selection
  • Environmental impact assessment
  • Feasibility studies
  • Permitting & regulatory guidance
  • Detailed engineering & design
  • Procurement & project management
  • Construction oversight
  • Risk management
