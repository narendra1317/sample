Infrastructure

Onshore buildings and energy infrastructure are vital to a reliable, efficient and sustainable energy supply. We provide the civil, structural and environmental engineering that gets them designed, approved and built.

What we do

From planning dossier to construction support.

We plan, design and manage infrastructure that supports industrial and energy development, and help clients secure approval from the authorities in line with codes, specifications and CDM regulations.

Chartered expertise. Our Managing Director, Ramesh Kumar, is a Chartered Engineer and Fellow of the Institution of Civil Engineers.

  • Civil & structural engineering
  • Site attendance & structural inspection, including reports
  • Design dossiers for local authority planning approval
  • RIBA design reports
  • Checking & approval of calculations
  • Approval of manufacturer-supplied products
  • Analysis & design of substructures and superstructures
  • Geotechnical analysis reports
  • Design of piles, rafts & isolated footings
  • Construction support
