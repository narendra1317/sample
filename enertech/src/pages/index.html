Capabilities

Nine disciplines. One point of accountability.

Our engineers work across disciplines from day one, which means fewer interfaces, fewer surprises and designs that are coordinated before they reach the yard.

  • 01Process

    PFDs, P&IDs, simulation, HAZOP and regulatory compliance.

  • 02Piping & layout

    Sizing, routing, isometrics and CAESAR II stress analysis.

    Rotating and static equipment, pressure vessels and PED calculations.
